**Why NMP is Important:**

“Let’s start with the big question: Why do we need a solution like NMP? Today’s campuses face a real challenge—managing multiple disconnected systems. Whether it’s in classrooms, auditoriums, or across an entire campus, juggling all of these systems separately can be time-consuming and prone to error. Imagine having to control every device—your displays, audio systems, and even your lighting—one by one. It’s inefficient and impractical. That’s why there’s a need for a smart, centralized solution to manage everything more effectively.”

------

**What NMP Does:**

“So, what exactly is NMP? It’s a smart management system that allows you to control all your devices from one central hub. NMP offers both **horizontal** and **vertical** integration, helping you manage and scale your technology infrastructure across different spaces and devices.

- **Vertical Integration:** In a single classroom or venue, NMP can connect with various devices—interactive flat panels (IFP), wireless presentation tools, media servers, recording systems, audio systems, and more. This creates a unified smart classroom solution. It allows deep integration of multiple systems within one space, ensuring seamless control from one interface.
- **Horizontal Integration:** Beyond individual rooms, NMP can scale up to manage multiple classrooms and even the entire campus. It integrates across different venues, enabling control of multiple systems from a central location. For larger spaces like auditoriums, NMP can pair with the CBX to extend wireless control over long distances via 2.4G Wi-Fi, making it easy to manage everything from one place. This broad integration across the campus allows NMP to function as the core of a smart campus ecosystem, including campus-wide control and broadcasting solutions.”

------

**How NMP Works:**

“Now, let’s talk about how the NMP works in practical scenarios:

- **In a Classroom (Vertical Integration):** The NMP simplifies control within a classroom. With one click, teachers can manage everything from the display to the sound system, making it easier to present, teach, and manage the classroom environment.
- **Across an Entire Campus (Horizontal Integration):** When it comes to managing an entire campus, this is where horizontal integration comes into play. NMP doesn’t just control individual rooms but can scale across the entire campus. You can manage devices in multiple classrooms or even connect with other IQ Ruida devices to handle tasks like campus-wide broadcasting or scheduling tasks for the whole day.
- **In Large Venues (Horizontal Integration):** For auditoriums or large spaces, the NMP paired with the CBX allows for long-distance wireless control. Imagine being able to adjust the sound or lighting from anywhere in the room, all from one interface. This level of control is crucial for events, presentations, or lectures in large venues.”

the usage of **horizontal** and **vertical** integration should be adjusted to align with your understanding:

- **Vertical integration** refers to deep integration within a specific system or room, like focusing on the classroom or a single venue.
